# 🌌 Project Afterlife

*An interactive storytelling and digital preservation experience for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 - "Abandoned but not forgotten."*

# 🧑‍🚀 Project Overview

Project **Afterlife** is not a textbook; it is an experience. It is a children’s story told from the perspective of “The Archivist”(the teacher) and the Mars Orbiter Odyssey(acting as "The Old Man"). Reaching the twilight of its operational life, Odyssey recounts its final tale to a young Cadet, chronicling the rovers and probes resting on the Moon and Mars.

This project was built by team Oopy-timism for NASA's 2026 Space Apps Challenge. It transforms complex engineering and planetary science into a poetic, gamified journey for classrooms and STEM outreach programs, backed by real science from NASA and JPL sources.

# 🧩 Structure of the Story

The project is structured into four distinct worlds:

Chapter I - The Archivist gives a hint, a forewarning.

Chapter II - The Odyssey: Reminisces its life, and shares its journey.

Chapter III - The Inevitable(Oppy's Final Days): A high-tension, interactive mini-game. The user desperately fights a Martian dust storm to save the Opportunity rover. The game is intentionally impossible. Facing this inevitable failure teaches a profound lesson on the unforgiving reality of space and the endurance of the human spirit.

Chapter IV - The Cliffhanger: The story transitions to the Voyager mission, ending with the haunting question: "OR ARE WE?". The Archivist takes a leap into the unknown, passing the baton to the next generation to write the unwritten chapters of our cosmic journey.

# 🛰️ Data Sources & Scientific Foundations
Every emotional beat is anchored in ground-truth reality. We utilized resources and datasets from the following NASA & partner archives:

NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) Missions

Mars Exploration Rover - Opportunity

NASA Mars Odyssey Orbiter

NASA Archives

These official sources inform the planetary imagery, hardware specifications, and environmental mechanics present in the interactive experience.

# Tech Stack

Built entirely from scratch without heavy game engines to ensure a seamless, cinematic web experience:

Core: Next.js and Typescript.

Rendering: Three.js.

Animation: Motion.dev.

# Innovation Highlights

📖 The Illusion of Control: Gamifying empathy through an unwinnable scenario (Chapter 3) to teach children about sacrifice rather than just victory.

🔭 Bridging Generations: Framing raw data as a grandfather-like tale passed down to a child.

🎨 Immersive Architecture: Swapping standard text corpora for high-tension UI, quick-time events, and 3D visual contrast.

📡 Open Ended Inspiration: Ending on a cliffhanger that explicitly challenges students to become the engineers of tomorrow.
